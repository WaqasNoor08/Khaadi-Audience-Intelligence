Which marketing patterns are unusual?

Each post is compared with posts of the same content type (robust z-score on views, video likes per view, comments per 1,000 views, negative, question and buying-signal share, and sentiment) and with an Isolation Forest. Posts need at least 10 collected comments and 1,000 views. Flags are leads to investigate, not proof of a problem.

About & limits

Data. 21,514 public YouTube comments collected on 12 Nov 2026 with the YouTube Data API v3 from 571 Khaadi clothing videos (perfume, "Khadi" herbal/stationery and IPO videos are filtered out). Video views, likes and publish dates come from the same API. All records are real; none are simulated.

Limits. One platform (YouTube); shares are not available; some videos feature several brands (Khaadi only appears in the title); topic rules and language detection are keyword based and Roman Urdu spelling varies; VADER under-scores Roman Urdu; cluster separation is weak, so segments are exploratory. Recommendations are hypotheses to A/B test, not proven effects.
